## How it is used

It is set behind the methods page as a faint ground at low opacity, under a paper-coloured veil, and it
is masked so that it fades out towards the page edges. It carries no information: the page makes no
claim that depends on it, it is hidden from assistive technology, and every piece of text on that page
still sits on an opaque surface with a measured contrast ratio.

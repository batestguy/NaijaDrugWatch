## Cannot be verified - do not print

1. **The "about 70% before, under 20% after" pair.** TIME gives 80% and no method; nothing read gives a
   post-2001 figure. If a page quotes a prevalence, it must quote TIME's 80% with the word
   "staggering" and say no method was given, or say nothing at all.
2. **The date of the attack.** TIME's "three years ago" from October 2005 implies 2002. The commonly
   repeated 2003 story may be a different incident. Do not date the attack.
3. **Her own account of her sister's death.** No direct quotation from her was found; TIME states it.
4. **Any criticism of her tenure.** Never searched. Do not claim there is none.
5. **Honours with dates.** "OFR" is confirmed by the NLN item; any list of awards is not.
6. **Exact birth place.** Appears in secondary sources as Makurdi; not in a confirmed source read here.
